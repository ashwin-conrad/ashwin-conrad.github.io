03 / FEATURED ENGINEERING PROJECT

ASSET INTEGRITY / ENGINEERING ANALYSIS

Brazed aluminum heat-exchanger lifecycle analysis

Operating data, OEM limits, and field context combined to support risk-based decisions on aging process equipment.

ROLE
Engineering Co-op
CONTEXT
AltaGas asset integrity
SCOPE
12+ heat exchangers
DATA WINDOW
2+ years of operation

ENGINEERING OBJECTIVE

Assess fatigue risk and monitoring gaps for brazed aluminum heat exchangers using historical operating data and OEM-prescribed operating limits.

CONTEXT

The equipment supports facility operations, so the analysis had to connect historical process behaviour with inspection planning and practical operational awareness.

MY CONTRIBUTION

Extracted process data with the PI Historian Excel add-in and developed Python screening tools for thermal ramp rates, cyclic operation limits, and parting-sheet temperature distributions.

DESIGN CONSTRAINTS

  • Historical-data quality
  • OEM operating limits
  • Sensor coverage
  • Aging equipment
  • Operational practicality

ENGINEERING PROCESS

  1. 01Extract operating data
  2. 02Screen against OEM limits
  3. 03Identify monitoring gaps
  4. 04Develop recommendations
  5. 05Present lifecycle findings

ENGINEERING DECISION

Use measured operating behaviour to focus the next action.

The work connected process data with failure mechanisms and monitoring coverage. Recommendations included additional instrumentation where coverage was insufficient and reduced actuated-valve opening rates to limit turbulent flow and erosion on the inner pipe face.

CURRENT RESULT / OUTCOME

  • Analyzed 2+ years of operating data across 12+ brazed aluminum heat exchangers.
  • Compiled lifecycle findings to support risk-based decisions, inspection planning, and operating awareness.

METHODS & TOOLS

PythonPI Historian Excel add-inData analysisTechnical assessmentEngineering reporting
